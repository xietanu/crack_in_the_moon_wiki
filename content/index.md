---
title: Kingdom of Verintal
---

Our adventure takes place in the Kingdom of Verintal. Once a prosperous kingdom, 73 years ago a cataclysm befell it - more than half of the population died from a mysterious and seemingly magical plague and, at the same time, monsters long hunted to extinction and undead rose from the earth, terrorising those left alive.

The Kingdom runs along the [[River Verin]], from [[Mount Kami]] at the north  to the sea in the south. The more densely populated south, including the capital city of [[Veringrav]], faired better during the recovery, but the more rural nort remains dangerous and isolated, with travel between settlements often risky.

This has led to groups of adventures setting up trade to do the dangerous and unpleasant work needed to keep others safe.
