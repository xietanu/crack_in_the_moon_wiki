This wiki sets outs the homebrew campaign setting of the [[Kingdom of Verintal]].

> This is provided as **OPTIONAL** background - you don't need to read any of it, just pick any bits that look interesting.

You can assume your characters have some familiarity with any/all information presented here - it is written to represent the understanding of an average citizen of Verintal. As such, not everything here might be true or complete.

## Setting

Once a prosperous kingdom, 73 years ago a [[cataclysm]] befell Verintal - more than half of the population died from a mysterious and seemingly magical plague and, at the same time, monsters long hunted to extinction and undead rose from the earth, terrorising those left alive.

The Kingdom runs along the [[River Verin]], from [[Mount Kami]] at the north  to the sea in the south. The more densely populated south, including the capital city of [[Veringrav]], fared better during the recovery, but the more rural north remains dangerous and isolated, with travel between settlements often risky.

This has led to groups of adventures setting up trade to do the dangerous and unpleasant work needed to keep others safe.
