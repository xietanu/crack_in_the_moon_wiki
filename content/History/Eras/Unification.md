---
title: Unification
---
**~400 years ago**

The pretext for the end of [[The Seven Kingdoms]] was the collapse of Valdenmarch.

When King Cranog Owen of Valdenmarch died without a named heir, his sister Braith and his nephew Cael both claimed the throne. The succession war that followed was not particularly bloody, but it disrupted grain supply across the north and sent refugees south along the river. For House Calsin in Abervin, watching from the estuary with the accumulated leverage of decades of preparation, this was both a problem and an opportunity.

Abervin crossed the Valdenmarch border with an army and framed it as a peacekeeping mission, which it clearly was not.

Cael Valden accepted Abervin's support and the vassalage that came with it. Braith fought on for two more years before her forces were broken at the **Battle of Greymere Ford** — the largest pitched battle in Verintal's recorded history — on the grey-watered steppe lake of the same name. Braith herself fled north with a handful of riders. She was never captured. Steppe tradition holds that she never died, only left.

From there, unification proceeded unevenly. Caer Derwyn formalised its existing marriage ties and joined early by treaty, its nobility largely intact. Dûrholm negotiated the **Dûrholm Charter** — guaranteeing guild autonomy over mining, reduced tithes, and the right to maintain a standing militia — rather than submit to conquest. The Keldmark Confederation calculated its interests and agreed to terms. Cwm Wesken resisted two military campaigns before capitulating, securing a guaranteed degree of autonomy over trade through the mountain pass. Only the Keepdom of Briganmere required no pressure at all: it needed Abervin's military protection and Abervin needed its spiritual authority to claim the right to govern the others. The deal they struck formally established the **Church of the Verin** as an institution with a role in state governance — the seed of [[the Church]]'s political power in the centuries that followed.

Abervin renamed itself [[Veringrav]] — *the river's grave* — a piece of calculated religious symbolism tying the new capital to [[Arawn]] and the gods. House Calsin became the founding dynasty of the [[Kingdom of Verintal]]. Their motto, *the river finds its course*, became the motto of the kingdom.

With the kingdom united, House Calsin turned its attention to consolidating power during the [[Building Century]].
