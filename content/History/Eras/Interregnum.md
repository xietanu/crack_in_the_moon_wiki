---
title: The Interregnum
---
**~170-150 years ago**

A succession crisis brought the kingdom close to fracture. Three claimants contested the throne over twenty years, none strong enough to win decisively. [[The Church]] positioned itself as neutral mediator, arbitrating disputes and quietly accumulating the debts all three claimants ran up. When the crisis resolved — through the death of two claimants and the Church-approved marriage of the third — the crown was weaker and the Church considerably stronger. The era is remembered in popular culture primarily through a popular if inaccurate cycle of ballads called [[The Three Kings' War]].

With the crisis over, [[The Quiet Years]] began.
