---
title: The Cataclysm
---
**73 years ago**

One summer night, the sky appeared to ripple with curtains of glowing light, as bright as midday. They appeared mostly green, though some are pink and purple, and they seemed to be blown by some powerful wind. Many people reported they smelt something acrid, like the taste in air after a thunderstorm. [[The Quiet Years]] were over.

The first deaths appear to have started the next day - it's hard to confirm many reports, but people reported seeing loved ones wither away in front of their eyes in minutes, all over the Kingdom. Whatever caused this seemed to spread like a plague, though for most people it took much longer for the disease to kill them entirely. No physician could identify the cause, while the kingdom's wizards made vague statements about a great damage to the Weave and [[the Church]] blamed it on a great divine punishment, though for what exactly it never really settled on. Many believe the gods were quieter after the cataclysm, less likely to speak directly to people, and no-one has credibly presented an explanation from them.

Whatever the cause, within three years it killed more than half the population. Neither rich nor poor were spared, with the plague killing even the Queen. The north, with its sparser infrastructure, was hurt worst. Entire villages disappeared overnight, their entire populations either dead or fleeing south. Food production collapsed, the first harvest almost entirely lost, which certainly contributed to the death toll. Then the monsters: things that had been absent from the valley for generations returned, and undead arose from fresh and century old graves alike.

Eventually, the plague just disappeared - people slowly stopped getting sick, dying. As much as with its beginning, no-one could explain its end.

That the kingdom survived at all is a small miracle. The credit for this is generally given to the Church and Queen Sela. The Church managed to maintain its networks across the country, coordinating responses and getting food and aid where it was needed. Queen Sela, before her death, presented a steady, brave and composed figure and with substantial work and sacrifice successfully held the kingdom's essential institutions together - something remembered in [[The Tragedy of Queen Sela]].

From this point, the only thing to do was begin the [[Reconstruction]].
