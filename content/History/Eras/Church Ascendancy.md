---
title: Church Ascendancy
---
**~200-170 years ago**

Following the [[Building Century]], a series of weak and short-reigning monarchs gave [[the Church]] the window it needed. Over roughly thirty years, a sequence of **Church Reforms** passed through the royal council — codifying tithing as a church tax, placing Church courts above local lord courts in "spiritual matters" and establishing the High Keeper of the Verin as a position with formal advisory authority to the crown. The last of the reform-era kings, Aldric III, died leaving a nine-year-old heir and a regency council half-composed of Church officials.
