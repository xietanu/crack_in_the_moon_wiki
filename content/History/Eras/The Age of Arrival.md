---
title: The Age of Arrival
---
**(~600–450 years ago)**

Human settlers arrived by sea at the [[River Verin]] estuary approximately 600 years ago. What they found was fertile, untouched land to build on and grow food. They came in waves, initially a few hundred, then thousands . The valley's natural fortification made it immediately attractive, and those who arrived first moved quickly to establish claims.

They soon met the current inhabitants of the lower valley: simple and nomadic halfling caravans who lacked the knowledge to properly cultivate the land. Friendly relations were established and an exchange followed, with the halflings, close to nature, teaching their knowledge of the land and the humans sharing their advanced technology and culture. The two cultures were soon combined, with halflings making up an important minority in what would become southern Verintal.

Further north, the settlers encountered a more savage foe. Orcs emerged from the hills and mountains to plunder human villages in what became known as [[The Karrath Raids]]. These ended peacefully, however, following some brave negotiating by a young prince and his marriage to the daughter of one of the orc war-chiefs, and the orcs retreated back into the mountains. The bravery and romance of this is celebrated in the famous musical, [[The Wandering Prince]].

With this, the settlers were now lived throughout the valley, and small kingdoms and city states started to solidify, beginning the era of [[The Seven Kingdoms]].
