---
title: Reconstruction
---
**Since 73 years ago**

The road back following the [[Cataclysm]] has been long, hard and slow. Eventually things started to stabilise and people worked their way back to normality, though things are still far from where they were. In the south, life can sometimes seem normal again, but this is not the case in the north - it remains sparsely populated with many abandoned settlements and dangerous roads, clearly bearing the scars.

Most people in the valley were now born after the Cataclysm, but those old enough to remember tend to show the trauma. Often this affects their relationships with the gods - some lost their faith, saying the gods abandoned them, some became religious fanatics to atone for whatever the kingdom has done wrong.
