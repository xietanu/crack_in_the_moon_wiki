---
title: The Building Century
---
**~300–200 years ago**

Following [[Unification]], the newly united kingdom established itself.

The first century of unified rule was the most visibly productive. The **King's Road** was paved — a stone-surfaced route running the length of the valley alongside the Verin, with bridges at every major crossing. Towns grew at crossroads. [[The Church]] built its network of shrines and temples across the kingdom; by the end of this period, every town of any size had a Church presence.

This age of development was interrupted by the [[Church Ascendancy]].
