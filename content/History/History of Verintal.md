# The History of Verintal

Scholars generally agree the history of Verintal can be broken down into the following eras:

1. [[The Age of Arrival]] - The arrival of humans in the valley around 500 years ago, when the land was first settled and developed. The end of this era is remembered in the popular piece of romantic musical theatre, [[The Wandering Prince]].
2. [[The Seven Kingdoms]] - The seven distinct polities emerged from the initial settlements, and existed in mostly stable equilibrium for around 150 years.
3. [[Unification]] - Following a succession crisis in the north that left their greatest rival weakened, the southern House Calsin led a campaign of diplomacy and war that united the Seven Kingdoms under one King - forming the [[Kingdom of Verintal]].
4. The [[Building Century]] - as the name suggests, for around 100 years, the Royal House of Calsin worked to establish the kingdom, building roads and infrastructure that still exist in the modern day.
5. The [[Church Ascendancy]] - A series of weak and short-reigning monarchs left the crown weakened. This era saw the first of the Church Reforms, which codified some significant privileges to the Church.
6. The [[Interregnum]] - A succession crisis that was the closest the kingdom came to failing until the [[cataclysm]]. The Church served as a neutral mediator, for which the resulting King Aldric IV was grateful. This period is captured in the dubiously accurate cycle of ballads [[The Three Kings' War]] and the drinking song [[Good King Aldric]].
7. [[The Quiet Years]] - As the name suggests, a quiet, prosperous time for the Kingdom. Trade flowed, harvests were bountiful and things were peaceful.
8. The [[Cataclysm]] - Lights in the night's sky were the first signal generally recognised, and their appearance is the date scholars use to mark the start of the Cataclysm. The earliest reports of deaths and the plague start the next day. Within 3 years, half of the kingdom would be dead, including the Queen.
   The kingdom's survival is generally credited to the Church, which used its networks to coordinate and provide aid, and Queen Sela before her death, who successfully held the essential institutions together - something remembered in [[The Tragedy of Queen Sela]].
9. [[Reconstruction]] - The road back following the Cataclysm has been long, hard and slow. While life in the south of the kingdom feels somewhat normal again, this is less so for the north, which still bears the scars.

Parallel to this is the history of the [[Monarchs]].
## The Present Day *(last 50 years)*

The north has never fully recovered. Abandoned villages and small towns dot the landscape north of Kindgart, some empty for decades, some more recently vacated. Monster populations remain elevated. The Church's influence has continued to grow, its relief networks hardened into permanent institutions that the crown depends on and cannot easily challenge.

The new generation — those born after the [[Cataclysm]] — have no memory of the world before. For them, this is simply how things are.
