---
title: River Verin
---
The River Verin runs from the northern tip of of the country, forming from the snow on [[Mount Kami]], and running all the way down to the sea. The Kingdom of Verintal sits in its valley.

The river is important in many respects to Verintal - providing fresh water to many towns and cities, as well as transport of goods and people through much of the country, it important in many practical, everyday ways. Beyond this, the river holds a strong religious importance to the kingdom. It is seen as symbolizing the cycle of life and death, and is assocaited closely with the goddess [[Arawn]].

The river also is the source of the names of many locations and towns. For example, [[Kindgart]] sits in the end of the 'childhood' portion of the river, and [[Veringrav]] sits on the estuary where it runs into the sea, or the at the grave of the river.
