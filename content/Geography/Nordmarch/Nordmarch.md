---
title: Nordmarch
---

The farthest north region of the [[Kingdom of Verintal]] is dominated by two features at opposite ends of it: **[[Mount Kami]]** and [[Kindgart]].

The landscape is mountains in the north, steppe in the west and taiga in the east, with for the most part sparse settlements. The locals know these as the Isenward range, the Windward Steppe and Ostholt, respectively. The final area most recognise is Kindgart, sitting on the banks of the great lake [[Calder Reif]], large enough and culturally different enough to be considered its own entity. 

There are two major routes through the region. Both pass through Kindgart:
- **The Kings Road** runs alongside the River Verin for most of its route, and in Nordmarch that means it extends along the east side of the valley from [[Kindgart]] to [[Wyrm's Roost]]. Near Kindgart it is a paved, maintained road but the further north you get,the less this becomes true, becoming a barely marked route by the time you get near the Roost.
- **The High Road** leads westward, running through the major settlements of the Windward Steppe into Stonebarrow, and carries a huge amount of the goods of the region to be shipped off southward from the docks in Kindgart.

## Subregions

### Kindgart and Calder Reif

The mountain is the source of the [[River Verin]], which pools in **[[Calder Reif]]**, a long highland lake of extraordinary clarity at Mount Kami's feet. The southernmost point of Calder Reif is generally considered the edge of Nordmarch. The Verin is only navigable for most boats south of the lake, making the north more remote and cut off from the rest of the kingdom.

Kindgart sits on the western shore of Calder Reif, and is the region's only real city. Almost everything that flows into or out of Nordmarch flows through Kindgart, making it a significant economic and cultural centre.

### Ostholt

East of the Verin, the landscape of Osholt is taiga — dense pine forests covering the foothills, where snow is common through winter and the ground is only reliably soft in summer. [[Glimwood]] is the only significant settlement, though many small villages dot themselves among the trees. Some of the villages are even still inhabited. Much of this area has been reclaimed by wilderness since the [[Cataclysm]].

### Windward Steppe

West of the river, the land opens into the great Windward Steppe — plains that transition from tundra in the north to fertile farmland further south. This is the kingdom's breadbasket, growing the wheat and barley that supplies much of Verintal. In every Inn in Nordmarch you'll find the black rye bread grown here, and can wash it down with some Greymarch Pale brewed by the dwarves of the region. Villages and towns are sparse, but have fared better than Ostholt given the kingdom's need for grain.

### Stonebarrow

In the far west and north is Stonebarrow, an area in the foothills and mountains rich in minerals and primarily settled by [[dwarves]]. A significant source of iron and metal for the kingdom, it is accessed by the **High Road**.
