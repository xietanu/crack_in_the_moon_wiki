---
title: Mount Kami
---
Mount Kami is the mountain that dominates the horizon at the northern end of Verintal, the tallest in its range and visible from almost everywhere in the kingdom. At a little over 8km tall, it is one of the tallest in the world, and has never been summited without magical assistance. The [[River Verin]] is born primarily from its snow, associating it sometimes with fertility and new life.

Despite being a harsh environment, the mountain is home to [[Wyrm's Roost]], a community of hardy orcs who have built a town into a sheer cliff face. Few have made the challenging journey to see it in person, but the town and culture of the orcs who life there have captured the imagination of many, leading to good luck charms and trinkets bearing old orcish runes and small, braided colourful 'orcish poetry knots' or 'mountain knots' being not uncommon sights around [[Nordmarch]].
