---
title: Morrigan
---
*The Phantom Queen. Goddess of War and Fate. Lady of Crows.*

Goddess of war, fate, death in battle, and transformation. The Morrigan is a shapeshifter and a prophetess — she often appears as a crow or raven before a battle, and her blessing (or curse) can decide its outcome. She is associated with courage, grief, and the hard truths other gods leave unsaid.

Symbol: Two crossed spears with a crow above.

Worshipped by: Warriors, soldiers, those who face hard choices. Also by those who value truth over comfort.
