---
title: Lugh
---
*The Long-Handed. Master of All Arts.*

God of crafts, arts, trade, and travel. Lugh is the jack-of-all-trades of the pantheon — a polymath deity associated with skill, cunning, and the use of talents in service of justice. Depicted as a young, radiant figure.

Symbol: A pair of long hands, open and upward-facing.

Worshipped by: Artists, traders, travellers, those who rely on their wits.
