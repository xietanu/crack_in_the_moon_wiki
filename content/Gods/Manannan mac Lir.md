---
title: Manannan mac Lir
---
*Lord of the Sea. Keeper of the Island Beyond the Sunset.*

God of the sea and storms. Manannan mac Lir is also associated with the Otherworlds — the places souls go when people die. While alive, people are in the care of [[Arawn]], and when they die she is said to hand them to Manannan.

Symbol: A crest of white water on dark green.

Worshipped by: Sailors, fishermen, coastal communities. Very common near Veringrav and the southern coast.
