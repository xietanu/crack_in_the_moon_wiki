---
title: Silvanus
---
*Lord of the Deep Wood. The Ever-Living.*

God of nature and the forest. Silvanus represents the parts of the world that don't care about human concerns — the forest that is older than kingdoms and will outlast them. In Verintal, where much of the north has been reclaimed by dangerous wilderness after the [[Cataclysm]], Silvanus can be a deity of both reverence and fear.

Symbol: A summer oak tree in full leaf.

Worshipped by: Rangers, druids, hunters, those living at the edges of civilisation.
