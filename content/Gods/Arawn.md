title: Arawn
---
*Goddess of life, death, and the cycle between. Lady of the River. The One Who Carries the Dead.*

Arawn governs the cycle of life and death — birth, aging, dying, and the passage of the soul into what comes after. The [[River Verin]] is sacred to her, understood as a physical manifestation of that cycle: born on [[Mount Kami]], flowing south through life and labour, meeting the sea at [[Veringrav]] ("the river's grave"). Arawn is depicted as a tall woman robed in grey and black, holding scales.

Given the link to the River Verin, Arawn is a central figure in the worship of [[the Church]], and an altar to her can be found in every Church shrine in Verintal.

Symbol: A star in a circle, with the lower point of the star showing waves in the shape of the [[River Verin]]
