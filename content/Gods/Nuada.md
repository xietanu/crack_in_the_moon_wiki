title: Nuada
---
*Silver Hand. King of the First Battle.*

God of war, legitimate rulership, and martial excellence. Where the Morrigan governs fate and transformation in battle, Nuada governs conduct and skill — the warrior's code, strength in service of others, and the right to rule.

Symbol: A silver hand on a black background.

Worshipped by: Military commanders, those who aspire to leadership, fighters who care about conduct and honour.
