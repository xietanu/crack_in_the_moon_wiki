---
title: Brigantia
---
*Lady of the Flowing Waters. Mother of Herds.*

Goddess of water, livestock, and everyday abundance. Where [[Arawn]] governs the sacred river, Brigantia governs the practical the practical application  — irrigation, clean water, the welfare of animals. A gentler, more domestic deity than most.

Symbol: A stone footbridge over water.

Worshipped by: Farmers, those who keep livestock, communities dependent on rivers for trade. Particularly worshiped around [[Lake Briganmere]].
