---
title: Dagdha
---
*The Good God. All-Father. The Great Cauldron.*

Father figure of the pantheon. The Dagdha governs the turning of the seasons, the abundance of the harvest, and the great rhythms of the natural world. Often depicted as a great bear of a man carrying a club and an inexhaustible cauldron.

Symbol: A bubbling cauldron over a shield.

Worshipped by: Farmers, landowners, those marking the turning of the year. His feast days align with the seasonal festivals.
