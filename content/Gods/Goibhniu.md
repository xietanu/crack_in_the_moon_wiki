---
title: Goibhniu
---
*The Master Smith. Father of the Forge.*

God of smithing, craftsmanship, and the kind of healing that comes from making things well. Goibhniu is also said to brew a divine ale that grants temporary immortality — a popular element of his mythology. Patron deity of many dwarves.

Symbol: A great mallet crossed with a sword.

Worshipped by: Blacksmiths, craftspeople, brewers. Common with dwarf communities.
