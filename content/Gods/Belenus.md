---
title: Belenus
---
*The Shining One. Lord of Light and Warmth.*

God of the sun, light, and the warmth of living things. Belenus is a guardian against darkness in both the literal and spiritual sense — the sun, but also the light of reason and clarity. Often invoked against undead and against the persistent shadow left by the [[Cataclysm]].

Symbol: A solar disk ringed with standing stones.

Worshipped by: Those who fear the dark, soldiers, healers. Popular in the north, where winters are long.
